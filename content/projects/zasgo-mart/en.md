## Overview

Zasgo Mart is a modern retail chain with 8 outlets in Labuan Bajo, Flores. As a store serving the daily shopping needs of both locals and tourists, Zasgo Mart needed an official platform to introduce the company, showcase the products it sells, and help customers find its stores easily.

This project was developed as a freelance job in the form of a company profile website built with WordPress. The website serves as the main information hub for customers, covering the company profile, a product catalog that includes fresh produce, groceries, and local Flores products, store location information, and ongoing promotions.

## Background

Retail businesses with multiple branches often face the same problem: scattered information. Customers have to ask the store directly or search through social media to find out which products are available, where the nearest outlet is, and when a promotion is running.

With 8 outlets in Labuan Bajo, Zasgo Mart needed a website that gathers all of this information in one place, looks professional, and is easy to access from both mobile devices and computers.

## Goals

- Introduce Zasgo Mart as a modern retail chain in Labuan Bajo
- Present product categories clearly, including local Flores products
- Help customers quickly find the location of all 8 outlets
- Provide a promotions page that is easy to update
- Build a professional and trustworthy impression for customers and potential partners
- Create a website whose content can be managed independently by the Zasgo Mart team

## My Contribution

I worked on this project as a freelance web developer and was involved in almost every stage of the process.

- Planning the page structure and navigation flow of the website
- Building the website layout using WordPress and Elementor
- Organizing content so that product, location, and promotion information is easy to find
- Making sure the layout is responsive across different screen sizes
- Filling in and refining the website content
- Preparing the website so the client can manage it after the project is complete

## Scope of Work

### Home page and company profile

The home page serves as the first impression for visitors. It presents the identity of Zasgo Mart, a summary of the company, and quick access to key sections such as products, locations, and promotions. The company profile page explains who Zasgo Mart is and its commitment to serving customers in Labuan Bajo.

### Product catalog

Products are grouped into categories that reflect customer needs, namely fresh produce, groceries, and local Flores products. This grouping helps visitors understand the types of products available without having to visit the store, while also giving local products more room to be discovered.

### Store locations

Zasgo Mart has 8 outlets in Labuan Bajo. The location page presents information for each outlet so customers can quickly find the nearest store.

### Promotions

The promotions page is designed to keep customers informed about the latest offers. Its structure is built to be easily updated by the Zasgo Mart team whenever a new promotion is available.

## Design Process

### Structure planning

The process began by mapping the information needs of visitors. I drafted a list of pages and decided on the order of the most important information: who Zasgo Mart is, what it sells, where its stores are, and what promotions are available.

### Visual design

The design is clean, modern, and easy to read, matching the image of a modern retail brand. Whitespace, heading hierarchy, and product image placement were carefully considered so that the pages never feel crowded despite holding a lot of information.

## Development

### WordPress as the foundation

WordPress was chosen for its flexibility and ease of management. The Zasgo Mart team can update content on their own without touching any code, such as adding a new promotion or updating product information.

### Elementor as the page builder

Elementor was used to build page layouts visually. With this page builder, I could arrange page sections consistently, adjust the display for desktop, tablet, and mobile, and speed up revisions whenever the client had feedback.

### Responsive on every device

Since many customers look up store information on their phones, the website was tested and adjusted to be comfortable to read on small screens, including the navigation menu and product catalog display.

## Challenges & Learnings

The main challenge of this project was presenting many types of information at once, namely the company profile, product catalog, 8 outlet locations, and promotions, in a layout that stays tidy and easy to understand. The key was grouping the content well and designing a clear navigation flow.

Another challenge was balancing the client's wishes with the needs of visitors. From this project, I learned that a company profile website for a retail business should start from the everyday questions customers have, not just from an attractive design.

Some lessons I gained:

- Content structure is just as important as visual design
- Client websites should be designed to be easy to manage after handover
- Clear communication with the client speeds up the revision process
- WordPress and Elementor are highly effective for company profile projects with limited timelines

## Result

The Zasgo Mart website was successfully launched and is accessible at https://zasgomart.com/. It serves as the official information platform featuring the company profile, product catalog, locations of 8 outlets, and promotions, making it easier for customers to get to know Zasgo Mart and find the nearest store.

This project also strengthened my experience handling freelance work from end to end, from planning to a live website used by the client.
